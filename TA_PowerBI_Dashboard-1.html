💡 Key Findings & Recommendations
🎯 Funnel Efficiency
Interview L2 stage has the highest drop-off. Recommend implementing structured interview scorecards and calibration sessions to reduce subjective rejections. Current L1→L2 conversion: 79%, L2→Offer: 70%.
🌍 Diversity Performance
Diversity ratio at 48% exceeds the 40% target. Chennai is below benchmark at 28.3% — focus campus drives and women-in-tech job boards in that location.
⏱ Time to Hire
Avg TTH is 43.8 days vs 30-day target. Recruitment Agency channel is fastest at 37.9 days. Consider building talent pools in advance for high-volume departments.
⭐ Quality of Hire
Marketing scores highest at 4.07/5. Campus hiring shows top quality score (4.0) with lower TTH — increase campus recruitment budget for FY25–26.
💰 Offer Acceptance
Overall offer acceptance at 67.5%. Female candidate acceptance is lower — investigate compensation equity and workplace flexibility to close the gap.
🏆 Best Source
Campus hiring delivers highest quality score (4.0) and Recruitment Agency delivers fastest TTH (37.9 days). Hybrid strategy: use agencies for speed-critical roles and campus for quality-first roles.
